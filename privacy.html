Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

NaviGo is a transportation management system for trucking carriers, operated by Navi Cargo Inc, 1139 N Arlington Heights Rd, Unit 418, Itasca, IL 60143, USA ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information NaviGo uses, why, and the choices you have.

Information NaviGo uses

  • Account information — the name and email address of the people who sign in, and their role in the company.
  • Business data you enter or upload — loads, customers and brokers, drivers, trucks and trailers, expenses, payments, documents (rate confirmations, bills of lading, permits, licenses, medical cards, inspection reports, invoices and statements).
  • Gmail data (only if you connect a Gmail account) — NaviGo reads messages and attachments that relate to the company's operations, such as oversize permits, fuel reports and toll statements, and sends emails (driver settlements, permits) only when a user presses Send.
  • Google Drive data (only if you connect Google Drive) — NaviGo reads the files in the folder you choose to sort company, truck and driver documents. It has read-only access.
  • Integrations you connect — for example truck positions, odometer and hours of service from an ELD provider.

How the information is used

Only to provide NaviGo's features to your company: to record and calculate loads, pay and costs, to fill in forms from the documents and emails you give it, to remind you of expiring documents and payments, and to send the emails you ask it to send. Documents and email attachments may be processed by Anthropic's Claude AI service to read the text and fill in the fields; they are not used to train AI models.

Google user data

NaviGo's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. In particular:

  • Google user data is used only to provide and improve the user-facing features described above.
  • It is not sold, not used for advertising, and not used to build user profiles.
  • It is not transferred to others except as needed to provide those features (such as the AI processing described above), to comply with the law, or with your consent.
  • No person reads it unless you ask us to for support, it is needed for security or to comply with the law.

Where it is stored and how it is protected

Each company's data is kept in its own database on the server that runs NaviGo. Google access tokens and other connection keys are stored encrypted and are never shown back in the app. Access inside a company follows the roles set by its owner or admin.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We share it only with service providers that help run NaviGo (for example the AI service that reads documents and the email or cloud services you connect), when required by law, or with your permission.

Keeping and deleting information

Information is kept while your company uses NaviGo. You can disconnect Gmail or Google Drive at any time in Settings, and revoke NaviGo's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. To have your data deleted, email info@navicargoinc.com.

Children

NaviGo is a business tool and is not meant for anyone under 18.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
