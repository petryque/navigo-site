The office of a trucking company, in one place

NaviGo is a transportation management system (TMS) for trucking carriers. It runs dispatch, accounting, the fleet, documents, safety and recruiting — and reads the paperwork for you.

Dispatch

Loads, rate confirmations read automatically, real profit per load and per mile.

Accounting

Driver settlements, payments calendar, fixed costs, statistics.

Fleet

Trucks and trailers, maintenance, fuel and toll statements.

Documents & safety

Company, truck and driver documents with reminders; inspections, claims, accidents.

People & recruiting

Drivers, owner operators, office staff; driver applicants from first call to hire.

Email & Google Drive

With your permission, NaviGo reads permits, fuel and toll statements from your Gmail and files documents from your Drive.

Who makes it

NaviGo is built and used by Navi Cargo Inc, a motor carrier based in Itasca, Illinois. Questions: info@navicargoinc.com.
